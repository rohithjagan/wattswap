# 🌟 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful
- Be collaborative
- Accept constructive feedback
- Focus on community growth
- Support fellow contributors

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Trolling
- Discrimination
- Publishing private information

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions violating this code of conduct.

Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent bans.
